Colour

One warm cream base, one dark ink, one maroon accent. The cream paper and the hairline share a single tone — the hairline doubles as the recessed fill for photo wells and quiet bands. Gold, red, green and orange are reserved for meaning, never decoration.

How to use the accent

Maroon is the “do this” colour: primary buttons, prices, the active tab, the current step. It appears at most twice in one viewport. Gold is brand warmth — stamps, eyebrows, the current status bar. Red is only ever a deal or a problem; green only ever “done” or “safe to eat”.

05

House rules

  1. One accent colour per viewport, used at most twice.
  2. Plus Jakarta Sans for dish names, headings and the brand voice — never for buttons or form labels.
  3. Never a broken image: real photo or the woven cream placeholder.
  4. Colour carries meaning — red = deal or problem, green = done or dietary-safe.
  5. Every tap target is at least 44 × 44 px, including the mobile bar and quantity steppers.
  6. Prices and order numbers always use tabular figures.
  7. A card carries a heading or a rule, never both — space does the separating.
  8. A label and its value share a line. Never stack a short label above the value it names.
  9. Help is a sentence with a link in it, not a card with a title and a button.
  10. Copy is plain English, sentence case, and never blames the customer.
  11. Reduced-motion is respected across all animation.
