Our story

A family kitchen, cooking for families.

Asian Taste is a small, family-run Vietnamese kitchen on Henley Beach Road. We cook the way we eat at home — fresh each day, seasoned the way you ask, and made to be shared across the table.

  • Made from scratch daily

    Broths, sauces and fillings prepared in-house, not the day before.

  • Spice your way, level 1–5

    Tell our staff your number. Default is 3 — choose lower if you're not sure.

  • Vegan or vegetarian on request

    Every dish can be made to suit — just note it for our staff when ordering.
