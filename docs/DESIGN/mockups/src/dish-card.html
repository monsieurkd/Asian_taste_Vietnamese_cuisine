What each part does

1
Media. 4:3 photo when we have one. No photo? A woven cream placeholder with the dish initials — never a broken image. A sold-out stamp sits bottom-right.
2
Badge. Popular (gold) or Deal (red). Maximum one per card, and never on the same dish as another badge.
3
Name. Plus Jakarta Sans, sentence case, never truncated. Two lines maximum on mobile.
4
Option hint. Only appears when the dish needs a choice — this is the single strongest signal that the card won't add instantly.
5
Description. One line, plain English, no adjectives doing heavy lifting.
6
Dietary tags. GF / Vegan / Spicy, each with its own coloured dot. Same order on every card.
7
Price + action. Price in maroon tabular figures. Action adapts: Add, Choose options, or disabled Sold out.
